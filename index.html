Diligence

Every check run on the company

Cognitive rehabilitation software for speech and occupational therapists, sold to hospitals. Each check is labelled by where it was verified: a public register, the customer, a regulator, or the company alone.

Past pre-seed

About $2.5M equity raised by the company's account, four Innovation Authority pilot-track awards from 2022 to 2025, and ₪385,435 in export support. That is why the recommendation is at seed.

Company pricing page

The unit is the paying therapist

£37.50 per therapist per month on the Pro plan. A fifty-therapist department would be about $28,000 a year. The Israeli contracts are far smaller because each covers a handful of seats.

Procurement records and company materials

The paying base is small and undisclosed

About ₪150,000 a year in approved ceilings across the active hospital notices. The company does not say how many therapists pay; my estimate is about 200. The 5,000 on its site is the sign-up count from its 2021 US launch.

!
Company pricing page

Two compliance steps in progress

NHS DTAC and a HIPAA business associate agreement are listed as in progress. Both gate sales to NHS trusts and US health systems.

Competition

The competitor owns the clinical evidence

Constant Therapy has a randomised trial (Frontiers in Neurology, 2021). Cognishine has published no outcomes yet. Constant Therapy sells mainly to patients, Cognishine to clinicians through institutions. The gap matters more as the company scales.

Decision
$500,000
at seed

After a first meeting, and only if three things check out: paying seats and revenue by customer, the cap table after the 2025 round, and NHS DTAC and HIPAA completed.

"The content is copyable in a day. Six sole-supplier records are not."The company leads with its content library. The stronger asset is six public hospitals that each certified it as the only supplier able to deliver. That status is renewed at every purchase, so it is a lead, not a lock.

How the numbers were built

The two calculations the memo rests on, so anyone can rerun them.

Pricing the outcome

Priced at the company's published Pro price, £37.50 per therapist per month, about $560 a year. Exit at eight times ARR, the middle of the range for vertical healthcare software with institutional customers, against roughly 4% ownership after dilution. The table shows whole scenarios rather than a decimal expected value, because the assumptions do not carry that precision.

Estimating paying therapists

The company does not disclose it. The estimate of about 200 divides the approved hospital ceilings by the list price and adds the therapists named in the company's UK case studies. The memo labels it as an estimate.

Limits

What this cannot do

It is a filter for which conversations are worth having, not a substitute for having them.

Public sources only

No data room, no founder meeting, no customer calls. In a fund, customer references would be the longest section of a memo like this.

Founders

Whether founders can be argued with is a reason funds decline, and it cannot be judged from outside. It is recorded in every file as the first thing to test in a meeting.
